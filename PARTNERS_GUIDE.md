# PrizeHub Ethiopia – Partner Programs & Commission Guide

Welcome to the official Partner Program of **PrizeHub Ethiopia** (under the sub-brand **abbaa carraa ethiopia shop**). This guide explains how our verified partners (Agents, Vendors, and Organizers) share their referral links, work collectively, and collect their commission.

## 1. Partner Program Roles & Split Structure

PrizeHub Ethiopia utilizes a transparent, automated **50/50 generated commission split** for its verified partners.

The platform generates a total commission of **20% of the pool's target value** (added to the pool's target amount to calculate the total seats). Out of this total commission generated, partners earn exactly **half (50%)**, which translates to **10% of the total target amount**.

| Partner Role | Program Application | Commission Earned | Setup Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Verified Agents** | Merkato VIP, City VIP & Regular Pools | **50% of Platform Commission** (10% of target value) | Direct customer promotion and seat registration. |
| **Verified Vendors** | Regular Pools | **50% of Platform Commission** (10% of target value) | List physical property (cars, houses, machinery) under PrizeHub accounts. |
| **Organization Organizers** | Internal Pools | **Custom Organizer Commission** (10% of target value) | Setup private pools for corporate/association members. |

## 2. How Referral Links Work

Every partner receives a unique, secure, tracking-enabled referral code and link upon verification.

### Sharing Your Referral Link
1. Log in to your **PrizeHub Ethiopia** account.
2. Go to your respective Partner Dashboard (Agent, Vendor, or Organizer Dashboard).
3. Open the **Referral & Commission Center** (`ReferralSystem` component).
4. Your unique tracking link will be displayed as:
   `https://prizehubethiopia.com/register?ref=YOUR_CODE`
5. Click **"Copy"** or use the one-click social share buttons to instantly post on **Telegram, WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok, or Twitter**.

### Automated Tracking Pipeline
- **Tracking Cookied Session**: When a client clicks your referral link, their browser registers your unique partner code in secure local/session storage.
- **Auto-Discount**: The invited member automatically gets a **5% to 10% discount** on their first seat contribution.
- **Instant Commission Allocation**: When the referred client registers and contributes to any Regular Pool, City VIP, or Merkato VIP seat:
  - The system checks the referrer's partner status.
  - Generates the transaction record and allocates the partner commission instantly into your **Pending Earnings**.

## 4. How Commissions Are Collected & Withdrawn

Commission settlement is fast, direct, and secure.

### Direct Custom Accounts for Creators (Open Your Shop)
Creators who open their own shop can input their **custom Bank Account (CBE / CBE Birr) and Telebirr number** directly during pool creation. Contributions and commissions for those shops bypass the platform escrow and are sent directly to their verified accounts.

### Platform-managed Pools (Escrow & Withdrawals)
For standard pools, City VIP, and Merkato VIP:
1. Contributions are securely kept under official PrizeHub Ethiopia bank/Telebirr details.
2. When the pool target is reached and the draw is completed, the partner's share is moved from **Pending** to **Available Wallet Balance**.
3. Partners can request a withdrawal directly from their dashboard to **Telebirr (0913277922)** or **CBE Bank (1000601091686)**.
4. Withdrawal requests are processed and transferred within **24 hours**.
